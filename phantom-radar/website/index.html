Install in 3 steps

  1. Press Download and open the file.
  2. Windows: follow the installer. Mac: drag the app into Applications, and the first time right-click it and choose Open.
  3. Turn Bluetooth on, launch Phantom Radar, and allow Bluetooth access if asked.
